Photo by Timelab Pro on Unsplash Introduction If you’re a developer, there’s no doubt that one of the most important questions you have to answer is “should my project be a monolith or microservice?”. Choosing an appropriate architecture is really hard, especially considering the fact that this decision may affect the entire life-cycle of the project. Additionally, making the right decision is often difficult as business requirements may change during the actual development.
